Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SELLB2, operated by Aexara Technologies Pvt Ltd, handles information when you use the SELLB2 website and mobile application.

SELLB2 is a marketplace for properties, vehicles and businesses. Users are responsible for checking ownership, title, approvals, dues, identity, condition and other transaction details before entering a transaction.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use SELLB2, we may collect and store:

  • Account information: email address, authentication information and an internal user/account identifier.
  • Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile, such as your name, phone number and profile information.
  • Listing information: titles, descriptions, prices, categories, property/vehicle/business details and location information that you enter when publishing a listing.
  • Photos and other user content: images you upload for listings or your profile.
  • Messages: messages and related information exchanged through SELLB2's in-app messaging feature.
  • Saved listings and account activity: listings you save and information needed to provide marketplace features.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve SELLB2, including to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • provide profiles, listings, search, filters and saved listings;
  • publish and display listings and user-provided content;
  • enable buyer-seller communication and messaging;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical problems;
  • respond to support requests and account-related requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Marketplace content and communication

When you publish a listing, the information and photos you choose to publish may be visible to other SELLB2 users so that the marketplace can function. When you use messaging, messages are transmitted to the intended conversation participants.

Do not publish passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers or other sensitive information unless it is necessary and lawful to do so.

4. Service providers and infrastructure

SELLB2 uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate the service. For example, authentication, database and file-storage functionality is provided through Supabase, and the website is hosted through Vercel. These providers process information on behalf of SELLB2 as necessary to provide the service and are subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Security

SELLB2 uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encrypted connections in transit and database access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SELLB2, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms. When an account is permanently deleted through the supported account-deletion process, the account and associated data are deleted as described on the account-deletion page, subject to information that may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

You can request account deletion at SELLB2 Account Deletion.

7. Children's privacy

SELLB2 is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Your choices

You may access and update information available through your account and may request deletion of your account using the account-deletion process. You should also keep your password confidential and notify us if you believe your account has been compromised.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SELLB2's features, processing practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy, account or marketplace questions, contact aexaratechnologies@gmail.com.
